Privacy Policy

Links (Android app, package com.aevrin.webview) · Effective October 30, 2026

Links is a personal Android app published by Aevrin. It opens a private, self-hosted website (gstorck.com) inside the app. It is distributed only to invited testers through Google Play and is not offered to the public.

What the app collects

The app itself does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or other device data.

What stays on your device

  • Cookies and website storage. Like a browser, the app keeps cookies and local storage set by the website so you stay signed in and your settings (such as the theme) are remembered. These stay on your device and are removed if you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
  • Files you choose. If you pick a file to upload, it is sent only to the website you are using in the app. Files you download are saved to your device's Downloads folder by Android.

The website the app opens

Using the app means using the website at gstorck.com and its sign-in service at auth.gstorck.com, both run by the publisher. As with any website, the servers receive the data needed to work: your IP address, the pages you request, the time of each request, your sign-in details, and anything you enter. Server logs are used only to operate and secure the service. This information is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Links to other sites

Links to other websites open in your regular browser, not in the app. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Google Play

The app is installed and updated through Google Play. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is available only to invited testers.

Your choices

You can clear the app's data in Android Settings → Apps → Links → Storage, or uninstall the app at any time. To ask about or delete data held by the website, contact the publisher below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
